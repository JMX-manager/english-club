About Us

More Than a Club

The English Club at Fushun No.1 Senior High is a student-led space where we drop the textbooks and actually speak. From movie nights to debate sessions, we create real opportunities to use English in ways that feel natural and fun.

No test prep. No grades. Just people who want to get better at expressing themselves in another language.

What We Do

Regular Activities

Something for everyone — from casual to competitive.

🎬

Movie Nights

Watch English films with subtitles, then discuss. Casual and fun.

🎙️

Movie Dubbing

Dub movie or anime clips — mimic voices and intonation for a fun way to level up your speaking.

📖

Reading Circles

Short stories, articles, poems. Read together, talk about what they mean.

🎧

Sing & Listen

Learn real expressions and linking from English songs — then sing them out loud.

✍️

Creative Writing

Flash fiction, journaling, poetry slams. Express yourself on paper.

🌍

Cultural Exchange

Connect with international pen pals and explore cultures through English.

🧠

Exam Ace Skills

Learn proven test strategies and study hacks from top English scorers. Build your foundation, boost your score.
